Northern Lights Relay — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Northern Lights Relay (“the App”) is developed by Ashutosh Bhardwaj. This policy describes how the current version of the App handles information.

Information stored on your device

The App stores your personal-best score, run count, and sound preferences locally on your device. The current prototype does not send this gameplay information to the developer or operate an account or game server.

Services and purchases

The current prototype does not include advertising, analytics, Game Center, or in-app purchases. If these services are added, this policy will be updated before that version is distributed.

Children’s privacy

The current prototype does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.
